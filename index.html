03 — Format

Tournament rules

RULE 01

Pool play

16 teams split evenly into 4 pools of 4. Round robin within each pool on that pool's own dedicated court — no switching courts mid-pool. Games to 11, win by 2.

RULE 02

Seeding

The top 2 finishers from each pool advance — 8 teams total — seeded 1–8 by pool finish and point differential.

RULE 03

Championship bracket

A clean 8-team single-elimination bracket — no byes needed. Quarterfinals, semifinals, and the championship final. See the full bracket below.

RULE 04

Scoring

Standard USAPA rally scoring rules apply. Self-officiated pool play; line judges for bracket rounds.

RULE 05

Equipment

Bring your own paddle. Loaner paddles and approved balls available at check-in.

RULE 06

Code of conduct

It's a charity event first, a tournament second. Good calls, good sportsmanship, good vibes.
