How this tournament works
The format

16 players · 4 teams of 4 · 4 groupings (foursomes), with one player from each team in every grouping. See the Groupings tab for tee times and live progress. Everyone plays their own ball. Full (100%) handicap is used for every player.

Scoring — Stableford points

Points are based on your net score (gross score minus the strokes your handicap gives you on that hole), not your raw gross score.

Double bogey or worse0 pts
Bogey1 pt
Par2 pts
Birdie4 pts
Eagle8 pts
Double eagle or better10 pts
Team scoring

On every hole, each team's best 2 individual point totals (out of that team's 4 players) count toward the team score for that hole — marked with a ★ on the Score tab. Add those up across all 18 holes; most total points wins.

Payouts

Every team is compared against every other team. The team with more points is owed the point difference multiplied by the stake (currently ${event.stake} per point), from the team with fewer points. The Money Leaderboard on the Payouts tab totals this up across all matchups for a single net number per team.

Access codes

Admins can edit the event, players, course, and every score. Each foursome has its own scorer code so they can only enter scores for their own group. The view-only code shows the leaderboard without editing.
