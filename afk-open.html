03 — Format

Tournament rules

RULE 01

Pool play

25 teams split into 5 pools of 5. Round robin within each pool — every team plays every other team in its pool. Games to 11, win by 2.

RULE 02

Seeding

Top teams from each pool advance to an 8-team single-elimination bracket, seeded by pool finish and point differential.

RULE 03

Knockout bracket

Single elimination, best of 3 games to 11. Quarterfinals, semifinals, and championship on featured courts.

RULE 04

Scoring

Standard USAPA rally scoring rules apply. Self-officiated pool play; line judges for bracket rounds.

RULE 05

Equipment

Bring your own paddle. Loaner paddles and approved balls available at check-in.

RULE 06

Code of conduct

It's a charity event first, a tournament second. Good calls, good sportsmanship, good vibes.
